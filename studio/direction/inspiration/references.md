## Design skills installed on this PC
- **frontend-design**: one bold thing, the rest quiet; no single accented word, no caps labels,
  no numbered markers unless it's a sequence; the AI-default palettes to avoid.
- **design-taste-frontend**: the AI-tells list (em dashes, startup-slop verbs, fake-perfect numbers,
  "the LILA rule": no default purple glow unless it is the brand, which for the house pack it is).
- **animate / emil-design-eng**: strong custom curves, never `ease-in` entrances, never scale(0),
  stagger 30-80 ms (motion.md uses the measured film values instead where they differ).
- **motionmaxxing**: everything in `system/never.md` and `motion.md`, plus `look.py`/`lint.mjs`.

## Berlin scene (web research 2026-10-13)
- Vanja Golubovic's Tresor/U8 posters: strict grids and plain type as order; colour and image
  play inside it (itsnicethat.com).
- Neale Johnston: type as an experimental material across formats (itsnicethat.com).
- Studio Gruhl, Spellbound identity: scanned ripped posters and street textures rebuilt as
  overlays, a modular collage system (itsnicethat.com).

  shadows and desaturate, so grade with that in mind (frame.io colorist interview).
