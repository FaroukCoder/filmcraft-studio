# The owner's taste

Two sources, kept apart so nobody mistakes a guess for a decision.

## Stated by the owner (decisions)
- **whatstheID's design language is theirs** (`Desktop/whatstheID/DESIGN.md`):
  "deep-field violet, chrome edges, hot pink accent." Two hues only: violet carries structure,
  pink carries attention. Never green. No pure white text. Edge-lit chrome, never flat fills.
  Michroma for anything that *names* a thing, JetBrains Mono for everything else. Lowercase UI copy.
  Every transition on one liquid curve `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`. Nothing hard-cuts *inside* the
  app (between scenes of a video, hard cuts are the pro default: see `motion.md`).
  Honesty over set pieces: no fabricated score or confidence.
- **Voice**: crates and vinyl. "drop the needle", "dig", "pressing the wax", "reading the grooves".
- **Music**: hypnotic techno and hardgroove; DJs on a Denon MC4000 with Serato.
- **v1 verdict (2026-10-13)**: the clips and promos read as stock AI. The text quality didn't match
  the footage; motion, colour and type had "no imagination" and didn't fit their personality.
- **Audience for the clip service**: DJ friends in Berlin, free, for traction and referrals.

## Inferred (confirm with the owner, then move up)
- The Berlin underground reads **restrained and typographic**: grids, plain type, colour and
  image doing the work inside a strict frame (Tresor/U8 posters by Vanja Golubovic; Neale Johnston),
  scanned street textures and early-internet collage (Studio Gruhl's Spellbound identity).
  Loud influencer captions read as the opposite of that scene.
- A DJ clip should look like it came from the night, not from an app: footage first, text small,
  real credits, a grade with grain you feel more than see.
- Each Berlin DJ is their own brand: the house pack is for the owner's channel only; clients get
  `club.css` until they give us their logo, type and references.

## One bold thing per video
Spend boldness in one place (frontend-design skill; motionmaxxing "emphasis is spent, not spread").
In a DJ clip that is the drop: the camera snaps and the name lands once. Everything else is quiet.
